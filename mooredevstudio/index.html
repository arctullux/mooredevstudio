Why should I hire a web developer?

Hiring a Web Developer to build your website can offer advantages for your business over using a website builder, or AI.

  1. A professional developer provides custom solutions tailored to growth.
  2. A developer can also make your site and brand identity look unique and pristine.
  3. Alex Moore will make your website stand out from competitors.
  4. Your website will have faster load times from cleaner code than a website made from a builder like Wix.
  5. A "no brainer" design. Your website will be easy to use.
  6. Correct and maximum SEO setup. The code is organized for search engines to crawl through your website.
  7. Alex Moore has full control over the code during the development stages, until the site is finished and handed over. This prevents issues before deployment.
  8. Your websites will be responsive. Everybody viewing it from a phone on portrait/landscape mode and from a computer can access and browse it just as easily.

Generative AI

Lets talk about the elephant in the room. Generative AI is impressive, but not perfect. It is not good at design or making systems that work with real people. You can always make a website with AI in minutes, but it will be a hassle to customize, update, and I haven't mentioned the potential hot water yet. All of this is because generative AI run by linear, predictable algorithms, meaning that its designs will come out generic, and commonly recognized as "AI slop".
